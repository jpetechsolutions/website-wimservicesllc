## Site build decisions

- **No fabricated content.** No invented testimonials, no made-up deal counts, no stock
  photos of people. Every number on the site traces to the table above.
- **Listings are not hardcoded** — they change weekly and 2 of 5 are already pending. The
  listings page sends buyers to his live BizBuySell/BizQuest profiles and pushes buyer
  registration instead.
- **Advisory is given its own section.** Exit planning, proformas, budgeting, HR and E-2 visa
  business plans are real services he offers that his current site barely mentions.
- **Compliance footer on every page** — brokerage licence, broker licence, CAM licences, the
  non–real-estate disclaimer for consulting, and a listing-information disclaimer.
- `/privacy` and `/terms` carry the A2P 10DLC language (exact required sentence about not
  selling SMS opt-in data, SMS Terms section, Msg & data rates, STOP/HELP) so the contact
  form can be used as an SMS opt-in later without a rewrite.
- Redirects in `public/_redirects` cover the old wbmconsulting.co URL structure.
