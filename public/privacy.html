Privacy Policy

How WIM Services, LLC collects, uses and protects information submitted through this website.

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Who we are

WIM Services, LLC (CQ-1074384), also doing business as WBM Consulting, is a licensed Florida real estate brokerage providing business brokerage and advisory services. Contact: warren@wimservicesllc.com or 360.904.4060.

Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us. Through the contact form that is your name, email address, phone number, the nature of your inquiry, and any details you add about your business, price range or timeline. If you contact us by phone or email we keep the correspondence and any information you provide in it.

Our web host records standard technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and reliability purposes.

How we use it

To respond to your inquiry, to provide brokerage and advisory services, to match buyers with suitable listings, and to meet our legal, licensing and record-keeping obligations as a Florida real estate brokerage.

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Confidentiality

Business brokerage depends on confidentiality. Information about a business for sale, including its identity and financial details, is released only to prospects who have executed a non-disclosure agreement and demonstrated financial capability. If you are an owner considering a sale, your inquiry is not disclosed to other parties.

How we share information

We share information only as needed to do the work or as required by law:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — website hosting and security
  • Our form delivery provider, which transmits form submissions to our email inbox
  • Our business email provider
  • Co-operating brokers, lenders, attorneys, accountants and closing agents, where necessary to progress a transaction you have engaged us on
  • Regulators, or others, where required by law or to protect legal rights

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies, remarketing pixels or cross-site tracking. If website analytics are added in future, this policy will be updated before they go live, and opt-out information will be provided.

Text messages

If you give us your mobile number we may call or text you about your inquiry. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of further messages, or HELP for help. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Retention

Inquiries are kept as long as needed to respond and to meet brokerage record-keeping requirements under Florida law. You may ask us to delete your information at any time by emailing warren@wimservicesllc.com, subject to records we are required to retain.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email warren@wimservicesllc.com.

Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The revision date at the top of the page shows when it last changed.
