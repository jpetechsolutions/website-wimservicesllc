Background

Thirty years on the other side of the table.

Over his career Warren has built, operated and sold fourteen businesses across the Main Street and lower middle-market sectors — having been both the buyer and the seller, he understands the decisions and the pressure on each side of a transaction.

Those ventures spanned construction, convenience stores, manufacturing, professional services, real estate, restaurants, retail and service-based companies. Each was created and grown hands-on, and each led to a profitable exit. Beyond his own companies, he has represented numerous owners and buyers through the complexities of getting a deal closed.

Today he works with business owners and buyers throughout Florida, focusing on Main Street and lower middle-market deals, and guiding clients from initial discussions through closing with a clear, organised and practical approach. The emphasis is on preparation, communication and execution — approaching each engagement with an owner's mindset, focused on the right outcome and on getting the deal done.

I approach each engagement with the same mindset I had as an owner. Warren B. Merrill
